2 · Adopt tonight, no vote needed

Parts of the package that are simply better than what we had

  • Protocol precision for sit-to-stand: cue-to-fifth-stand timing, event time at the first qualifying frame, the timer never pauses on tracking loss, explicit INCOMPLETE, INTERRUPTED, INVALID outcomes with a null time and a reason, every attempt preserved. Goes into briefs D1 and D2 and the protocol JSON.
  • The deliberate failure demo: occlude tracking, show the flag, withhold the time. Becomes a required demo step in Workstream H.
  • The evidence bar: three consecutive end-to-end runs, ten held-out clips with independent annotation, exact counts and time within 0.5 s, p50 and p95 latency with warm-up and sample counts, an assessment and export with networking disabled. Becomes Workstream G acceptance.
  • The record schema (`schema_version`, attempt, status, QC flags, data source, model hash, backend, software version). Goes into brief E1's storage and export.
  • Claim controls from the regulatory document: allowed today, allowed after testing, never. Becomes the rule for every sentence in the presentation.

The package's own ROI model returns a negative return at its default inputs (about minus 64 percent). Leave it out of the pitch unless the inputs are defended. The synthetic biomarker context is easy to misread as evidence; the package itself says keep it brief and after the live demo.
