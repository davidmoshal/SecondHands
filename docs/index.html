SecondHands prototypes

Interactive mock-ups and reference pages for the on-device movement assessment platform. The camera feed and keypoints in the prototypes are simulated; the counting and scoring rules are the real design. The repository Wiki is the source of truth.

Live: your camera, a real pose modelOpens your webcam, runs MediaPipe Pose in the browser, draws the skeleton, and counts chair stands or squats with the same knee-angle rule, in Home or Clinic view. Nothing leaves your device. Companion: Home and Clinic viewsOne chair-stand engine, two audiences: a large-type Home view with one instruction at a time, and a detailed Clinic view with overlays, per-rep measurements, editable thresholds, and notes. Clinic console prototypeSession, clinical tests (single-leg stance with live sway), progress, protocols, and device status. Architecture walkthroughData flow, the two-process layout for the Snapdragon NPU, model choices, the Qualcomm handoff, the interface contract, and the two-day plan. Project briefUse cases, end-to-end process, interface sketches, clinician-owned benchmarks, ownership, and reach. Team workflowProject structure, separation of concerns, Git rules, sharing the Tailscale-connected laptop, and where each model comes from.
Published from the docs/ folder of the SecondHands repository through GitHub Pages. Thresholds shown anywhere are placeholders until the clinicians set them.
